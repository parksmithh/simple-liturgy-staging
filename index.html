About the reader

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Scripture text?

Appointed lesson citations always appear. In Settings, Scripture text can stay Off (citation only), or show public-domain World English Bible (WEB) or King James Version (KJV) body text for Morning and Evening lessons. Some deuterocanonical lessons are absent from WEB; those keep the citation with a short note.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Load or install the app once while online so the reader, prayers, lectionary, and Scripture packs can be saved. It will then work in airplane mode, though reconnecting occasionally allows updates and restores data if your browser clears stored files.

What is Beginner's Apprenticeship?

Beginner's Apprenticeship creates inter-denominational books, music, podcasts, and simple liturgies that adapt historic Christian prayer rhythms for families and households with small children.
